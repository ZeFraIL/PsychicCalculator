# 📌 Engine & Helper Classes Documentation (English)

### 1. PsychicEngine
- **Role:** Pattern prediction engine logging keypress history and predicting next buttons.

### 2. MysticalAiEngine
- **Role:** AI commentary engine responding to division by zero, expenses, and round numbers.

### 3. SituationalContextEngine
- **Role:** Environmental awareness engine listening to light sensor, battery state, and witching hours.

### 4. TarotDeck
- **Role:** Model deck managing 8 astral Tarot cards with bilingual titles, emojis, meanings, and advice.

### 5. AstralVoice
- **Role:** TextToSpeech wrapper tuned to pitch 0.42 and speech rate 0.60 for deep eerie whispering.

### 6. MysticalAudioEngine
- **Role:** Real-time PCM audio synthesizer generating ghost wails, magic chimes, and error glitch noises.

### 7. EyeTrackingAnalyzer
- **Role:** CameraX ImageAnalysis analyzer using ML Kit Face Detection to map head angles to 4 screen quadrants.

### 8. ParticleSparkView
- **Role:** Custom View rendering particle explosion sparks on Canvas.

### 9. HapticPulseEngine
- **Role:** Vibrator helper delivering heartbeat waveforms and haptic feedback.
